Questions

Is ReelSaver free?

Yes. This demo has no subscription or payment feature. Hosting providers may impose free-tier limits.

Can I download private posts?

No. This service only attempts to retrieve publicly accessible posts and does not bypass login or access restrictions.

Why might a reel fail?

Instagram can block automated requests or change its systems. A public link is not a guarantee that a download will work.

Where does my file go?

Your browser normally saves it to your Downloads folder. On Android, check Files → Downloads.
